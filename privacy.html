Privacy Policy

Little Cues · Last updated 13 September 2026

The short version

Little Cues has no account, no server and no analytics. What you log stays on your phone. We collect nothing.

What the app stores

Your baby's name and date of birth, the feeds, sleeps, nappies, pumping sessions and medications you log, any notes you add, and the name you choose to show next to your entries. All of it is stored on your phone, inside the app's own storage, and is included in your device backups encrypted the same way the rest of your phone is.

Where it goes

  • Nowhere. The app makes no network connections. We do not run a server and hold no copy.
  • Sharing a day or exporting: only when you tap Share. The text or file goes wherever you send it.
  • Apple Watch: if you use the watch app, what you log on the watch is sent to your phone over Apple's own connection between your paired devices. It does not pass through us, and the watch keeps no store of its own.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifiers, no third-party software of any kind. Apple may show us aggregate, anonymous App Store statistics if you have opted in to sharing them with developers at the iOS level; that is between you and Apple.

Notifications

Optional and local to your phone. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else to make them work.

Rating prompt

The app may ask, once, whether you would rate it on the App Store. That request is Apple's standard one and goes to Apple, not to us.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything from your phone. Deleting the app does the same.

Feedback

Send feedback opens an email to us with only the app version and iOS version filled in. You choose what else to say.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with the date it changed. It will never change in a way that makes "Data Not Collected" untrue.
