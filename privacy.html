Privacy Policy

Little Cues · Last updated 25 September 2026

The short version

Little Cues has no account, no server and no analytics. What you log stays on your phone unless you share it with your partner, and then it goes through your own iCloud. We collect nothing.

What the app stores

Your baby's name and date of birth, the feeds, sleeps, nappies, medication and pumping you log, any notes you add, and the name you choose to show next to your entries. All of it is stored on your phone, and in your iCloud only if you share with your partner.

Where it goes

  • With sharing off (how the app starts): nowhere. The app makes no network connections. We do not run a server and hold no copy.
  • With sharing on: your records sync through your own iCloud using Apple's CloudKit, and through your partner's once they join. What you log (names, times, amounts, notes) is stored encrypted there; only record identifiers, the kind of entry, and when each record was created, changed or deleted are not. We have no server and can't read any of it. The people you invite see and can change everything for the babies you share, including notes and medication.
  • Invitations are Apple's own share links, sent however you choose. We never see anyone's email address or phone number. Predictions, Guide bookmarks and your settings stay on each phone.
  • Sharing a day or exporting: only when you tap Share. The text or file goes wherever you send it.
  • Apple Watch: if you use the watch app, your phone and watch talk over Apple's own connection between your paired devices, not through us. The watch keeps what it shows (your baby's name, the last feed and sleep, today's counts and the next windows) and anything logged on it until your phone has it.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifiers, no third-party software of any kind. Apple may show us aggregate, anonymous App Store statistics if you have opted in to sharing them with developers at the iOS level; that is between you and Apple.

Notifications

Optional and local to your phone. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else to make them work.

The guide

Guide articles are included in the app. Saved articles and preparation checklists stay on this phone and are cleared by Delete all data. Source links and videos open externally only when you tap them. Those services make their own connections and their privacy policies apply. We do not attach your baby's records to these links.

Rating prompt

The app may ask, once, whether you would rate it on the App Store. That request is Apple's standard one and goes to Apple, not to us.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything from your phone and, with sharing on, from your iCloud as well, which ends your partner's access. A record someone else shared with you is left, never deleted for them. Removing someone from sharing stops their future access; it can't take back what is already on their phone or anything they exported. Once nobody else is sharing, you can stop using iCloud on this phone; what is already there stays until you delete it. Deleting the app clears this phone but not your iCloud copy, so use Delete all data first, or remove it under your name → iCloud in the Settings app.

Feedback

Send feedback opens an email to us with only the app version and iOS version filled in. You choose what else to say.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with the date it changed. It will never change in a way that makes "Data Not Collected" untrue.
