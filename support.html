Little Cue

A feed, sleep and nappy log for the first year or two, with a likely window for the next feed or sleep worked out on your phone from your baby's own pattern. No account, no server, no analytics.

Get in touch

Questions, bug reports and requests are all welcome. Email csergay@gmail.com and you will get a reply.

Inside the app, Settings → Send feedback opens the same address with the app and iOS versions filled in and nothing else.

What it is not

Little Cue does not do growth charts, milestones, photos, solids or sleep programs, and it never compares your baby with anyone else's. It records what you tell it and shows patterns. It is not medical advice. With any concern about your baby, contact your child health nurse, GP or emergency services.

Common questions

How do I log something that already happened?
Hold the Feed, Sleep or Nappy button, or tap "Earlier…" under the buttons, and set the time.
Why is there no prediction yet?
The card appears once there is a feed or a sleep to measure from. With few entries the window leans on what is typical for your baby's age; add the date of birth in Settings so the age is right. As your own entries build up the window follows them instead.
How do I get the widgets?
Hold an empty spot on the home screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and find Little Cue. Quick log puts the three buttons on the home screen; Next up and Since last also fit the lock screen.
Does it work on Apple Watch?
Yes. Install the watch app from the Watch app on your phone. The watch shows the three buttons and complications; it keeps no store of its own and sends what you log to the phone.
Can my partner see the record?
Not in this version. Every entry shows who logged it, so whoever picks up the phone knows where things stand. Sharing between two phones is being worked on.
How do I get my data out?
Settings → Export gives you a CSV or JSON of every entry through the share sheet. Share a single day as plain text from the share button on the main screen.
How do I delete everything?
Settings → Delete all data removes everything from your phone. Deleting the app does the same.
Night mode is red. Is that on purpose?
Yes. Deep red on black does not light a dark room. It follows the hours you set in Settings, or a switch.
