Little Cues

A feed, sleep and nappy log for the first year or two, with a likely window for the next feed or sleep worked out on your phone from your baby's own pattern. No account, no server of ours, no analytics. Share with your partner through your own iCloud if you choose.

Get in touch

Questions, bug reports and requests are all welcome. Email csergay@gmail.com and you will get a reply.

Inside the app, Settings → Send feedback opens the same address with the app and iOS versions filled in and nothing else.

What it is not

Little Cues does not do growth charts, photos, solids or sleep programs, and it never compares your baby with anyone else's. It records what you tell it and shows patterns. Neither the log nor the guide is medical advice. With any concern about your baby, contact your child health nurse, GP or emergency services.

Common questions

How do I log something that already happened?
Hold the Feed, Sleep or Nappy button, or tap "Earlier…" under the buttons, and set the time.
Why is there no prediction yet?
The card appears once there is a feed or a sleep to measure from. With few entries the window leans on what is typical for your baby's age; add the date of birth in Settings so the age is right. As entries build up, the recorded pattern carries more weight in the blend. From 52 corrected weeks, milk-feed windows use recorded gaps alone; there is no feed window until gaps have been logged. The dots show recorded history, not predictive reliability; the score appears alongside average window width.
How do I get the widgets?
Hold an empty spot on the home screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and find Little Cues. Quick log puts the three buttons on the home screen; Next up and Since last also fit the lock screen.
Does it work on Apple Watch?
Yes. Install the watch app from the Watch app on your phone. The watch shows the three buttons and complications. It works through your phone: it keeps a copy of what it shows and holds anything you log until your phone has it.
Can my partner see the record?
Yes, if you invite them. Settings → Share with your partner sends an invitation by Messages, Mail or a link. You both need an iPhone signed in to iCloud. Once they join, you both see and log the same record. Logging works offline; changes send when iCloud is reachable. Predictions and Guide bookmarks stay on each phone.
How do I stop sharing?
Settings → Share with your partner → Manage who's invited, then remove someone or stop sharing. A partner can leave the shared record from their phone. Removing someone stops future access; it cannot take back existing copies or exports. Once nobody else is sharing, you can stop using iCloud on this phone; the iCloud copy stays until deleted.
What is the guide?
Short articles for each stage, from getting ready for the birth through the first year, each with its public health source. Open it from the book button on the main screen. It reads offline, and source links open only when you tap them. It is general information: with any concern, contact your child health nurse, GP or emergency services.
How do I get my data out?
Settings → Export gives you a CSV or JSON of every entry through the share sheet. Share a single day as plain text from Days → Share.
Can I restore a backup?
Settings → Restore a backup accepts a Little Cues JSON export and previews what will be added. Matching records are skipped; existing edits and deleted entries are kept. CSV is for spreadsheets. With sharing on, restored entries for shared babies can sync through iCloud and new babies join the shared record.
Can I simplify the Now screen?
Settings → Compact Now screen replaces the dial with status lines. Feed and sleep windows can be switched separately, including their pending notifications and widget/watch windows.
Can I share an appointment summary?
Days → Share → Summary for an appointment lets you choose up to 90 days and optionally include entry notes. Check the preview before sharing. Days without counts are identified and excluded from averages.
How do I delete everything?
Settings → Delete all data removes everything from your phone and, if you share, from your iCloud as well, ending your partner's access. A record someone else shared with you is left, never deleted for them. Deleting the app clears the phone but not iCloud: use Delete all data first, or remove the copy under your name → iCloud in the Settings app.
Night mode is red. Is that on purpose?
Yes. Deep red on black does not light a dark room. It follows the hours you set in Settings, or a switch.
